Privacy Policy

Daily News Caricature · Effective date: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily News Caricature Android app and website (the "App") handle information. The App is developed by Romas Peleckis ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The App shows a daily editorial caricature and a short news summary.
  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or other personal files.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The App only reads published caricatures and news summaries, which are stored with our hosting providers Supabase (database and image storage) and GitHub Pages (website). Like any web server, these providers may log technical data such as your IP address, device/browser type and request time for security and operational purposes. See the Supabase Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The news summaries and images are generated automatically by AI services from public news sources. No user data is sent to these AI services.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, ad views, clicks, crashes)

This data is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Data sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties, except for the data that the Google AdMob SDK collects as described above, or when required by law.

Data security and retention

All data exchanged between the App, our hosting providers and Google services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests relate to Google; you can manage them in your Google Account and device ad settings. You can also contact us for help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
